## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-precision cold-chain integrity, editorial elegance, and modern logistics transparency. It serves artisanal ice cream creators, dairy purveyors, and high-velocity cloud kitchens navigating hyperscale platforms such as Swiggy and Zomato.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Clean Editorial SaaS meets Tactile Frost & Precision Telemetry.
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm pristine milk tones (`#F8FAF7`) juxtaposed against deep preservation evergreen (`#123D32`), clinical mint signals (`#65B88A`), and high-energy lime accents (`#C7F36B`).
- **Emotional Intent:** Pristine sanitary control, unbroken sub-zero chain of custody, enterprise-grade reliability, and culinary craftsmanship.
- **Tactile Details:** Micro-frosted borders, ultra-fine borders mimicking vacuum-sealed glass, clinical sans-serif data displays, and dynamic telemetry status chips.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical hygiene and rigorous food preservation without resorting to generic industrial blues.

- **Primary (`#123D32`):** Deep Forest Green. Anchors top-level authority, primary CTAs, high-density data tables, and navigation bars.
- **Secondary (`#65B88A`):** Fresh Spearmint. Highlights operational health, stable temperature corridors, inventory vitality, and secondary flows.
- **Tertiary (`#C7F36B`):** Kinetic Lime. Used sparingly for critical conversion milestones, quick-commerce live pulses, and high-priority metrics.
- **Neutral (`#17211D`):** Near-Black Spruce. Delivers high contrast for editorial titles and body typography while preserving warmth.
- **Surface Dark (`#0B241E`):** Deep Abyssal Green. Dedicated to dark mode sections, night shift telemetry boards, and fulfillment status dashboards.
- **Canvas (`#F8FAF7`):** Warm milk-white background that eliminates clinical sterility in favor of refined culinary poise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances consumer-facing clarity with technical monitoring fidelity.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Sculptural, geometric curves bring an open, premium feel to editorial statements and marketing statistics.
- **Body Text (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and structurally consistent for multi-column manifestos, SLA specifications, and delivery audit logs.
- **Labels & Telemetry (Manrope):** Geometric precision engineered for dense status indicators, temperature dials (`-18°C`), batch timestamps, and quick-commerce SLA countdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy utilizes an open, architectural 12-column fluid grid designed for heavy inventory tables, split telemetry charts, and clean operational metrics.

### Breakpoints & Columns
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Single-column stacked telemetry cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Dual-card inventory layouts.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, `margin` (3rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Max container width capped at 1440px with balanced breathing room.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical rhythms favor airy, unhurried breathing space (`space-xl` between sections), while component internals follow strict, compact multiples of 4px to maintain density in active hub displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages cold-chain transparency: translucent frosted glass, razor-sharp perimeter lines, and low-viscosity ambient shadows.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Flat Base (Canvas):** Pure `#F8FAF7` surface with zero elevation.
2. **Surface Low (Tier 1 Cards):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` layered over the canvas, surrounded by a subtle hairline border (`1px solid #E2EAE5`). Casts an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.04)`.
3. **Surface Medium (Frosted Floating Badges & Popovers):** Translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), paired with a multi-stop ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.08)`.
4. **Surface High (Drawers, Modals & Alerts):** `#FFFFFF` paired with `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(11, 36, 30, 0.16)`, framed by a crisp border (`rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.08)`).
5. **Dark Chambers (Hub Terminal View):** `#0B241E` container with inner hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and high-contrast ambient green haloing.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates refined, geometric curvature (Level 2 Roundedness). 

- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons:** Rounded at `0.5rem` (8px) to project structured confidence.
- **Content Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px), echoing the smooth profile of modern refrigeration units and pristine packaging.
- **Badges, Pills, and Real-Time Telemetry:** Fully rounded capsules (`9999px`) to emphasize live sensor tracking and dynamic SLA verification.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#123D32`, text in `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner glow. Hover triggers smooth transition to `#0B241E` with a faint `#C7F36B` bottom-edge emphasis.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E2EAE5` outline. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAF7` and text to `#123D32`.
- **Accent Action:** Background in vibrant `#C7F36B`, typography in `#0B241E` (`Manrope` Bold). Used for high-priority dispatch and onboarding triggers.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- **Cold-Chain Sensor Badge:** Pill container with translucent mint fill (`rgba(101, 184, 138, 0.15)`), micro-dot indicator pulsing in live green, typography in `label-telemetry` displaying live metrics (e.g., `-19.2°C STABLE`).
- **Integration Pill:** Logos for Zomato / Swiggy enclosed in pill badges with neutral background and 1px border.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2EAE5`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#123D32` with an outer ring of `3px rgba(101, 184, 138, 0.2)`. Label renders in `Manrope` Medium.

### Cards & Metrics Modules
- **Editorial Hub Cards:** Crisp white background, `1rem` radius, hairline border, internal metric rows separated by fine rules (`#F1F5F2`).
- **Dark Inversion Cards:** `#0B241E` background, stark white value readouts, muted mint descriptive text, and a glowing Lime (`#C7F36B`) micro-sparkline.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp custom controls using `#123D32` on active states with crisp white checks; inactive borders use `#69766F`.

### Temperature Range Slider & Cold Gauge
- Precision track with dual gradient points, displaying safe thermal zones (`-22°C` to `-14°C`) via soft gradient transitions and high-contrast numerical markers.
