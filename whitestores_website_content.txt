Section 2 — What Is Quick Kitchen?

A kitchen operations partner, not another food brand.

WhiteStores provides the kitchen infrastructure, equipment and operational team required to prepare partner-brand products against customer orders.

The brand supplies the food products and defines the preparation SOPs. We handle the kitchen execution.

Products are prepared and fulfilled under the partner brand's identity.

________________________________

Section 3 — What We Can Do

Thaw
Controlled thawing according to brand instructions.

Heat
Heating and reheating ready-to-eat products.

Fry
Frying products according to prescribed preparation methods.

Grill
Grilling and finishing food items.

Blend
Blending beverages, ingredients and suitable food products.

Finish & Pack
Final preparation, packing and handover according to agreed specifications.

Actual capabilities depend on the menu, equipment and operational setup at the location.

________________________________

Section 4 — How the Model Works

01. You supply the products
The brand provides its food inventory. WhiteStores does not purchase the food inventory.

02. You define the SOPs
The brand provides the recipes, preparation instructions and product specifications.

03. We manage the kitchen
WhiteStores arranges the required equipment, kitchen staff and operating setup based on the menu.

04. Orders trigger preparation
Food is prepared only against incoming customer orders.

05. We prepare and fulfil
Our team follows the brand's SOPs, completes the preparation and manages the agreed packing and handover process.

06. Your brand reaches the customer
The food is sold under the partner brand's name. WhiteStores remains the operational partner behind the kitchen.

________________________________

Section 5 — A Flexible Operating Model

Built around your requirements.

Depending on the commercial arrangement, WhiteStores can support different kitchen operating models.

Brand-Led Model

The brand provides the products and SOPs. WhiteStores manages the kitchen infrastructure and preparation operations.

WhiteStores-Managed Model

WhiteStores manages the kitchen operations and execution under an agreed arrangement, while the food products and brand ownership remain with the partner.

Both models are designed around order-based preparation and partner-defined product specifications.
