Operational Audit

Dark Store vs. White Store

Why gourmet brands choose Propelpad White Stores over cramped conventional dark warehouses.

⚠️ Conventional Dark Store
  • ✕ Cross-Contamination Risk: Frozen food sits next to laundry detergents, raw onions, and spices.
  • ✕ Temperature Fluctuations: Frequent door openings with standard chest freezers causing thermal spikes to -6°C.
  • ✕ High Product Spoilage: Recrystallized gelato, collapsed ice creams, damaged packaging resulting in negative reviews.
  • ✕ Zero SKU Traceability: Expired lots shipped due to lack of strict automated FIFO/FEFO barcode control.
✨ Propelpad White Store Standard
  • ✓ Hospital-Grade Cleanroom Flooring: Seamless anti-bacterial white epoxy flooring with zero odor cross-absorption.
  • ✓ Automated -18°C Telemetry: Continuous IoT temperature sensors with automated alerts and dual DG emergency failover.
  • ✓ Pristine Product Integrity: Micro-climate insulated dispatch staging areas prevent melting before rider handover.
  • ✓ Automated FEFO / FIFO Picking: Every jar & carton scanned via optical barcode reader preventing aged stock leakage.
