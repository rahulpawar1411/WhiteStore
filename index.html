Operational Audit

Dark Store vs. White Store

Why gourmet brands choose Propelpad White Stores over cramped conventional dark warehouses.

Before

Conventional Dark Store

  • 01
    Cross-Contamination Risk

    Frozen food sits next to laundry detergents, raw onions, and spices.

  • 02
    Temperature Fluctuations

    Frequent door openings with chest freezers causing thermal spikes to -6°C.

  • 03
    High Product Spoilage

    Recrystallized gelato, collapsed ice creams, damaged packaging.

  • 04
    Zero SKU Traceability

    Expired lots shipped without automated FIFO / FEFO barcode control.

After

Propelpad White Store

  • 01
    Hospital-Grade Flooring

    Seamless anti-bacterial white epoxy — zero odor cross-absorption.

  • 02
    Automated -18°C Telemetry

    IoT sensors, alerts, and dual DG emergency failover.

  • 03
    Pristine Product Integrity

    Insulated dispatch staging keeps product cold until rider handover.

  • 04
    FEFO / FIFO Picking

    Every jar & carton scanned — aged stock never leaves first.
